Frequently Asked Questions

How does Christmas Countdown Widget work?
Christmas Countdown Widget uses your device's local time zone and system clock to calculate the exact time left until Christmas morning or the New Year. It shows the remaining days, hours, minutes, and seconds and updates continuously. You do not need an account or any setup. Open the app and the countdown starts.
Does the app need an internet connection?
No. The countdown is calculated on your device using your local clock and time zone, so Christmas Countdown Widget works offline. Your device may still use a connection for its own clock sync and for app updates from the store.
Why does my countdown look different from a friend's?
Christmas Countdown Widget counts down to Christmas and the New Year in your own local time zone. A friend in a different time zone, or with a different device time setting, will see a different remaining time. This is intentional, so the countdown always matches midnight where you are.
Can I count down to the New Year as well as Christmas?
Yes. Christmas Countdown Widget can show the time remaining until Christmas morning or until the New Year. The available targets are shown in the app.
Is Christmas Countdown Widget battery friendly?
Yes. The app is a lightweight, minimalist MVP with a reduced workload and a simple interface. It is designed to use as little battery as possible while still showing a smooth, live countdown.
Do I need to create an account?
No. Christmas Countdown Widget does not require an account, sign-in, or personal information. Open it and start counting down.
What happens after Christmas or New Year passes?
Once a target date has passed, Christmas Countdown Widget moves on to the next upcoming occurrence based on your device's date, so the countdown is always ready for the next holiday season.

Troubleshooting

The countdown shows the wrong time
Check that your device's date, time, and time zone are correct. On most devices, turn on automatic date and time in Settings. Christmas Countdown Widget always follows your system clock, so correcting the device settings will correct the countdown.
The widget is not updating
Home screen widgets refresh according to your operating system's schedule, which can limit how often they update. Open the app to see the live, second-by-second countdown. If the widget seems stuck, remove it and add it again from your home screen.
The seconds appear to skip or stutter
Close other apps running in the background and make sure Low Power Mode or Battery Saver is off, because these modes can reduce animation smoothness. Restarting your device may also help.
The app closes unexpectedly
Update Christmas Countdown Widget to the latest version from the App Store or Google Play, and make sure your device's operating system is up to date. If the problem continues, uninstall and reinstall the app, then email us with your device model and OS version.
The countdown changed after I traveled
This is expected. Christmas Countdown Widget uses your current local time zone. When your device switches time zones, the countdown adjusts to show the time remaining where you are now.
