Christmas Countdown Widget
A simple countdown that respects your privacy.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how exlinetr ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use Christmas Countdown Widget (the "App") on your mobile device.

Christmas Countdown Widget is a minimalist countdown app that calculates the time remaining until Christmas and the New Year using your device's local clock and time zone. We designed the App to work with as little information as possible. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Definitions

The following terms are used in this Privacy Policy:

  • App: the Christmas Countdown Widget mobile application, including any home screen widgets it provides.
  • Application Provider, We, Us, Our: exlinetr, the developer and provider of the App.
  • You, User: the individual who downloads, installs, or uses the App.
  • Device: the smartphone, tablet, or other device on which you use the App.
  • Personal Data: any information that identifies, or could reasonably be used to identify, an individual.
  • Platform Provider: the operator of the app store through which you obtained the App, such as Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play).

In Short

Christmas Countdown Widget does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, or location. The countdown is calculated entirely on your device.

Information We Collect

Christmas Countdown Widget is built to work on your device. We do not ask you to provide Personal Data in order to use the App.

Information Processed on Your Device

To display the countdown, the App reads your device's current date, time, and time zone settings. This information is used only on your device to calculate the time remaining. It is not sent to us.

  • System date and time
  • Local time zone setting
  • App preferences, such as your selected countdown target (Christmas or New Year), stored locally on your device

Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not request access to, and we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Precise or approximate location data
  • Photos, camera, microphone, or contacts
  • Account credentials or payment card details

No Access to Your Photos or Personal Files

Christmas Countdown Widget does not access your photo library, camera, files, or contacts. The App only needs your device's clock and time zone to work.

How We Use Information

The limited information processed by the App is used only to:

  • Calculate and display the time remaining until Christmas or the New Year
  • Remember your in-app preferences on your device
  • Keep home screen widgets up to date
  • Understand and fix crashes or performance issues, if you have chosen to share diagnostics with developers

No Selling or Advertising Profiles

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles.

Analytics and Diagnostics

We do not run our own tracking or advertising analytics in Christmas Countdown Widget.

Your Platform Provider (Apple or Google) may provide us with aggregated, anonymized statistics such as download counts, crash reports, and app performance data. On iOS, this is shared only if you have enabled "Share with App Developers" in your device's Analytics settings. On Android, similar data may be provided through Google Play Console based on your device settings. These reports do not identify you personally, and they are governed by the Platform Provider's own privacy policy.

You can turn off diagnostic sharing at any time in your device settings.

Data Retention

Preferences stored by Christmas Countdown Widget stay on your device until you change them or delete the App. Uninstalling the App removes its locally stored data from your device.

Aggregated diagnostic reports provided through Platform Providers are kept only as long as reasonably necessary to keep the App stable and to improve it, subject to the Platform Provider's retention policies.

If you email us, we keep your message and email address only as long as needed to answer your request and for reasonable record-keeping, after which it is deleted.

Data Security

Because the countdown is calculated on your device and we do not collect Personal Data through the App, there is very little information that could be exposed. We still follow reasonable practices to protect the App and any correspondence you send us.

  • No user accounts or passwords are stored by us
  • No transmission of countdown data to external servers
  • Support emails are handled only by authorized personnel

Your Data Stays on Your Device

Christmas Countdown Widget does not upload your clock, time zone, or preferences to our servers. Keeping your device's operating system up to date and protected with a passcode adds further protection.

Children's Privacy

Christmas Countdown Widget is suitable for a general audience, including families. We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from children under 13, or under the minimum age required in your country.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us Personal Data, for example by email, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your Personal Data. Because the App does not collect Personal Data, most information can be managed directly on your device.

  • Change or reset your preferences inside the App
  • Turn off diagnostic sharing in your device settings
  • Delete the App to remove all locally stored data
  • Email us to request deletion of any correspondence we hold

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we add new features to Christmas Countdown Widget or if laws change. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, we may also notify you within the App or on the store listing.

If you continue to use the App after changes take effect, you accept the updated Privacy Policy.
